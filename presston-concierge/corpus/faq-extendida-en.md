### 1. Why steel?
Steel is the active sector today: a real operation already executed end to end, with full traceability and AI-governed administration — every movement is analyzed and defined with AI for continuous improvement. Other sectors are under technical study.

### 2. Is this passive, or do I have to work?
It's not passive. Partners contribute capital **and** working time co-managing departments (portfolio and clients, marketing, among others). You acquire a stake with real management duties. It's not buying shares or a financial product offered to the public.

### 3. When do I get my money back?
Recovery in 12 months: through the monthly profit distributions you recover what you contributed. Your capital keeps working: the base stays in the operation and your stake appreciates as the company grows. Returned on wind-down: at the end of the program your capital is returned.

### 4. What is the scale of the operation?
29 containers in year 1, 36 per city at regime. City 2 in year 2, city 3 in year 3. Program term: 36 months, with reviews at month 12 and 24.

### 5. Is the round still open?
The Steel Round target is $120,000. Minimum ticket: $25,000. The amount committed to date is the current ROUND_COMMITTED value; the remainder is the difference against the target.

### 6. How is my capital protected?
Each operation is an independent LLC with a defined, private group of partners under written agreement. Capital is held in a restricted-purpose account under independent escrow / custody. And you see everything: real-time carrier tracking, cameras at the processing center and on distribution vehicles, and an accounting platform with real-time POS.

### 7. What if I want to exit?
It's an orderly exit: your stake is offered first to the partners, then to the company, then to an approved buyer, with objective valuation and staged payment.

### 8. What makes Presston different?
Three things: a real operation already executed (not an idea on paper), AI-governed administration with continuous data analysis, and full traceability of capital and goods. These are projections from an operating plan, not guarantees.

### 9. What happens after this conversation?
The private evaluation: a direct conversation to see if there's mutual fit. If you'd like, I'll send you the transcript of everything we discussed — where would you prefer to receive it, email or WhatsApp?
