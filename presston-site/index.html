08 · FAQ

How it works, answered.

What does it mean to be a partner?

You become a member of an independent LLC created for a specific trade operation. You hold a real stake, take on real responsibilities, and take part directly in how it runs. Membership is private and by agreement. It is not buying shares, and it is not a financial product offered to the public.

How do I track the operation?

Through our traceability platform. You get real-time shipping tracking from the carrier, live cameras and tracking at our processing center and distribution vehicles, and an accounting platform with POS that shows figures in real time. As a member you always know where the merchandise is and how it is being settled.

How and when can I exit or withdraw?

Your participation runs on the terms set in your agreement, which define how and when you can withdraw and how settlement works, including the option to settle in raw material. Everything follows the cycle of each operation and what you and Presston agreed in writing.

Is it legal? How is it structured?

Each operation is an independent, legally structured LLC with a defined group of members who join by agreement. Membership is private and not open to the general public. Member capital is held in a restricted-purpose account under an independent third-party escrow agent. As with any decision involving your capital, we recommend consulting your own legal and financial advisor.

How much do I need to participate?

It depends on the operation and is defined together, as part of your agreement. Because membership is private, participation terms are arranged directly with each member, not offered publicly.

Does Presston guarantee returns?

No. Results come from real trade operations and can vary; we do not guarantee returns. What we do is study each operation thoroughly and manage risk actively, so our members can take part with clarity and confidence.
